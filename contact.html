Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How accurate are the tax calculations?
Our calculations are based on the official Finance Act 2025 tax slabs. We update them after every Union Budget. For complex situations (capital gains, business income), consult a CA.

Q: Do you store my financial data?
No. The income data you enter is used only to compute your tax in real time and is never stored on our servers.

Q: Which financial year does this calculator cover?
This calculator covers FY 2025-26 (Assessment Year 2026-27).

Q: Can I use this for capital gains or business income?
Currently, this calculator is optimized for salary and regular income. Capital gains, business income, and other special provisions require a more detailed analysis with a CA.
