Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. Introduction

TaxCalcIndia ("we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our income tax calculator.

2. Data We Collect

Income data you enter is used only to compute your tax estimate. We do not store your financial data on our servers. We may collect anonymised usage data (page views, browser type) via Google Analytics to improve the service.

3. Payments

PDF report payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card or banking details. Razorpay's privacy policy governs payment data. After payment verification, we generate and deliver your PDF report.

4. Cookies

We use a cookie to remember your theme (dark/light mode). Google AdSense may set cookies for personalised ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Google AdSense

We display ads via Google AdSense. Google may use data for ad personalisation. Opt out at adssettings.google.com.

6. HuggingFace AI

When you request an AI summary, anonymised tax figures are sent to the HuggingFace Inference API for summarisation. No personally identifiable information is shared.

7. Security

All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS. Razorpay payment signatures are cryptographically verified server-side.

8. Children

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, visit our Contact page.
