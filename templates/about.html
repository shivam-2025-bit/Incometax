About TaxCalcIndia

Making Indian tax simple since 2024

Our Mission

TaxCalcIndia was built to make Indian income tax understandable for every taxpayer. Whether you're a salaried professional, a freelancer, or a business owner — you deserve a fast, accurate, and free way to understand your tax liability without needing a finance degree.

What We Offer

  • Free Old vs New regime comparison for FY 2025-26
  • Accurate slab-wise breakdown with 87A rebate, surcharge & cess
  • AI-powered plain-English summary via HuggingFace
  • Downloadable PDF report (₹500) — professional & CA-shareable
  • Deductions: 80C, 80D, HRA, NPS, standard deduction
  • Age-based exemptions for senior and super-senior citizens
  • Dark mode, mobile-first, fast-loading

Technology

Built with Python (Flask) backend, vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend, Razorpay for payments, HuggingFace Inference API for AI summaries, and ReportLab for PDF generation. Hosted on Render via GitHub CI/CD.

Accuracy

Our tax engine is built from Finance Act 2025 notifications. We update slabs every year after the Union Budget. Results are estimates — always verify with a CA before filing.
