About TaxCalcIndia

Helping Indians calculate tax since 2024

Our Mission

TaxCalcIndia was built with one goal: make Indian income tax calculations simple, transparent, and accessible to every taxpayer — whether you're a salaried professional, a freelancer, or a business owner.

Understanding your tax liability shouldn't require a finance degree. We believe every Indian deserves a free, accurate, and easy-to-use tax calculator that compares Old vs New regime and helps you make informed financial decisions.

What We Offer

  • Accurate income tax calculation for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)
  • Side-by-side comparison of Old and New tax regimes
  • Section 87A rebate, surcharge, and health & education cess calculation
  • Support for major deductions: 80C, 80D, HRA, and more
  • Age-based exemption limits (below 60, senior citizens, super seniors)
  • Slab-wise tax breakdown for full transparency
  • Mobile-friendly, dark mode supported interface

Why Trust Us?

Our calculation engine is built directly from the Finance Act notifications and CBDT circulars. We update our tax slabs every year after the Union Budget announcement. While we take every care to ensure accuracy, we always recommend verifying your final tax liability with a Chartered Accountant before filing.

Our Technology

TaxCalcIndia is built using Python (Flask) on the backend and vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend — keeping it lightweight, fast, and accessible on any device or internet connection speed. The site loads in under 2 seconds and works on all modern browsers.
