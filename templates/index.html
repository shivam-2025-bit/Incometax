Understanding Indian Income Tax for FY 2025-26

The Indian income tax system offers two regimes for FY 2025-26. The New Tax Regime provides lower rates with a standard deduction of ₹75,000 and a full rebate under Section 87A for income up to ₹12 lakh, making it beneficial for most salaried individuals. The Old Tax Regime allows various deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, NPS) and may save more tax for those with high eligible deductions.

New Tax Regime Slabs (FY 2025-26)

₹0 – ₹4L: Nil | ₹4L – ₹8L: 5% | ₹8L – ₹12L: 10% | ₹12L – ₹16L: 15% | ₹16L – ₹20L: 20% | ₹20L – ₹24L: 25% | Above ₹24L: 30%. Section 87A gives full rebate if income ≤ ₹12L.

Old Tax Regime Slabs

₹0 – ₹2.5L: Nil | ₹2.5L – ₹5L: 5% | ₹5L – ₹10L: 20% | Above ₹10L: 30%. Senior citizens (60+) get ₹3L basic exemption; super seniors (80+) get ₹5L.

Surcharge & Cess

10% surcharge above ₹50L, 15% above ₹1Cr, 25% above ₹2Cr, 37% above ₹5Cr. A 4% Health and Education Cess applies on tax + surcharge.
